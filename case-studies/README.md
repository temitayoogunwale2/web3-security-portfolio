# Sanitised Security Case Studies

## Purpose

This directory is intended for security case studies that can be shared publicly without violating confidentiality, engagement terms, or responsible-disclosure requirements.

## Research Experience

I have used WRRE, my research engine, in more than 15 audit or security-assessment engagements involving smart contracts, protocols, and wallet/cryptographic systems. The underlying records are mostly confidential.

This statement describes my reported research experience; individual engagements will be documented publicly only where the evidence and disclosure permissions support publication.

## Publication Standard

A case study may be published only when:

- The disclosure is authorised and any applicable embargo has been respected.
- Confidential code, target identities, secrets, and sensitive details are removed.
- The analysis and evidence status are described accurately.
- Any claimed vulnerability has reproducible supporting evidence.
- Limitations and remediation status are made clear.

## Case Study Template

Each approved case study should explain:

1. The general system type and authorised scope.
2. The security property or risk investigated.
3. How WRRE-assisted analysis and manual review were combined.
4. The reproduction method and observed behaviour.
5. The impact and limitations supported by the evidence.
6. The remediation approach and whether the fix was tested.
7. The disclosure status and any necessary redactions.
